Cookies

Fiscontable (fiscontable.app) · Last updated: November 10, 2026

In short: we use only the cookies needed for you to sign in securely. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, which is why you do not see a cookie banner.

Cookies we use

All of them are strictly necessary: without them you cannot sign in and the service cannot be protected.

What is stored in your browser

Besides cookies, Fiscontable stores preferences in your browser (local storage) so the app looks the way you left it: the active company, theme, table columns and filters, and a mark that you already signed in on that browser so we can take you straight to your dashboard. That information does not leave your browser.

How we measure visits

To know how many people visit the site we use Cloudflare Web Analytics. It uses no cookies, stores nothing in your browser and does not follow you across sites: it only counts visits in aggregate (page views, country and device type).

What we do not use

  • Advertising or social media cookies.
  • Tracking pixels.
  • Tools that record what you do on screen.

How to delete them

You can delete cookies and local storage from your browser settings. Doing so signs you out and resets your preferences. More on how we handle your data in the Privacy notice.
