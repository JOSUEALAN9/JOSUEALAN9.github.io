For Windows

SAT procedures, from your own PC

Fiscontable Agent is a program that does on your computer what you request on the web: with your internet connection, your browser, and a copy of every document on your machine.

  • Runs in the background, with an icon next to the clock.
  • Keeps a copy sorted by taxpayer ID in Documents\Fiscontable.
  • Your PC calls the server: it opens no ports and accepts no connections.
  • You approve each PC and can disconnect it at any time.

FAQ

Before you start

Do I need to install anything?

No. Fiscontable runs in the browser. The Agent for Windows is optional: it lets SAT procedures run on your own computer.

What do I need from each client?

Their e.firma (the .cer and .key files and their password) or their SAT password, depending on the procedure.

Where are e.firmas kept?

Encrypted on Fiscontable's server, and used only for the procedures you request. In the demo they are not stored.

Can I work with my team?

Yes. You create your firm, invite each person and decide which companies and modules they see.

Does it work on Mac or on a phone?

The web app does, in any current browser. The Agent is only for Windows 10 and 11.

What does the demo include?

The main modules, so you can get to know the system with your own companies for 7 days. When you subscribe, every module in your plan is activated and you keep what you already did.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the number of companies and people. Request your demo and we will share the plan that fits you.

Is the app in English?

The application itself is in Spanish, since it is built for accounting work in Mexico. These public pages are available in English.
